Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

netops is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google scope gmail.modify), netops reads unread emails in the owner's Gmail account that match a configured filter, and marks them as read after processing. It does not send, delete or forward emails.

How data is used

Email subject, sender and body are used only to detect and analyse network events. For analysis, this content is sent to the AI model providers configured by the owner (Anthropic and OpenRouter). Results are stored in the owner's own database and posted to the owner's team chat.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with anyone else. Google user data is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
